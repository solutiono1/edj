# RESEARCH

Evidence gathered while building. Used to decide what to build and whether the market is real. Sources are cited by name and date where available.

## Market

- B2B is the leading stablecoin use case. One analysis puts B2B at roughly $226 billion, about 60 percent of global stablecoin payment volume, growing 733 percent year over year.
- Card network data reports $401 to $527 billion in stablecoin payments from January to August 2026, up 42 to 63 percent year over year.
- A 2026 survey found 95 percent of respondents expect growing stablecoin interest over the next six to twelve months, with B2B applications leading among current users.
- Privacy is a named 2026 trend: configurable privacy by default, selective disclosure, and hidden amounts.

## BCH facts

- CashFusion can mix BCH but does not mix CashTokens. Stablecoins on BCH are CashTokens, so private stablecoin payments have a real gap.
- BCH has CashShuffle and CashFusion for BCH privacy, but stablecoin privacy tooling is thinner.
- BCH gained Pay-to-Script, bounded loops, functions, and bitwise operations in the May 2026 upgrade. Native introspection opcodes from 2022 enable covenants. This opens a path to on-chain constraints.
- There is active research on confidential transactions and post-quantum shielded pools for BCH and CashTokens using ZK-STARKs verified in CashVM covenants.

## Implications for pagor

- The demand for private business and payroll payments is real and growing.
- The unique wedge is BCH-native CashToken privacy, not a copy of EVM privacy pools.
- The strongest path is: CashTokens covenant privacy with real proofs, targeted at the BCH freelancer and treasury ecosystem.

## Open risks

- BCH stablecoin liquidity is much smaller than USDC and USDT on other chains.
- Real zero-knowledge privacy on BCH is still research, not a shipped product.
- Compliance and issuer policy for private business payments remain unresolved.
- The current prototype is a demo with signed statements, not yet a defensible privacy engine.
