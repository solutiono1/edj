Legal

Terms, privacy and data.

Plain terms for a software prototype. edj is not a bank, an exchange, or a custodian. You keep control of your wallet.

Terms

edj is software for creating private payment links, payroll drafts and signed disclosure proofs on Bitcoin Cash. It is provided as a prototype for evaluation. There is no warranty, no service level, and no custody of funds.

Settlement happens on a public chain and is visible. edj reduces what is published for specific flows. It does not make a payment anonymous, and the product never claims that it does.

Disclosure proofs are signed statements with a named subject and an expiry. They are not zero-knowledge proofs and do not prove a balance beyond the threshold in the statement.

Privacy

edj runs without analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app stores an edj session token in localStorage after Google sign-in. No cookie is set, and there is no cookie banner because there are no tracking cookies.

Google Identity Services is used only to verify your identity when you choose to sign in. Google receives the standard sign-in request for that interaction. Wallet payments are made from the wallet you choose; edj does not hold your keys.

Link, payroll and proof state lives in memory on the server for this prototype. It is lost when the server restarts. Notifications are generated in the same process.

Delete your data

To request deletion of any data held by the prototype, email hello@edj.cash from the Google address you used. You can also clear the edj session token from localStorage at any time by logging out or clearing site data.

No email marketing is sent. There are no subscriptions to unsubscribe from.

Third parties and age

The only remote service required for sign-in is Google Identity Services. The local HTMX script is served from this site. Wallet connection is delegated to the user's chosen BCH wallet. No reviews or testimonials are displayed.

The prototype is not intended for children under 13.

Edj is a software project for Bitcoin Cash. Settlement is public, details are private. This is not anonymity.
