Terms, privacy and data.

Plain terms for a software prototype. pagor is not a bank, an exchange, or a custodian. You keep control of your wallet.

Terms

pagor is software for creating private payment links, payroll drafts and signed disclosure proofs on Bitcoin Cash. It is provided as a prototype for evaluation. There is no warranty, no service level, and no custody of funds.

Settlement happens on a public chain and is visible. pagor reduces what is published for specific flows. It does not make a payment anonymous, and the product never claims that it does.

Disclosure proofs are signed statements with a named subject and an expiry. They are not zero-knowledge proofs and do not prove a balance beyond the threshold in the statement.

Privacy

pagor runs without analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics SDKs. The app stores a pagor session token in localStorage after email or Google sign-in. No cookie is set, and there is no cookie banner because there are no tracking cookies.

Email passwords are hashed with bcrypt on our server. Google Identity Services is used only when you choose to sign in with Google. Google receives the standard sign-in request for that interaction. Wallet payments are made from the wallet you choose; pagor does not hold your keys.

Link, payroll and proof state lives in memory on the server for this prototype. It is lost when the server restarts. Notifications are generated in the same process.

Delete your data

To request deletion of any data held by the prototype, email hello@usepagor.xyz from the email address you used. You can also clear the pagor session token from localStorage at any time by logging out or clearing site data.

No email marketing is sent. There are no subscriptions to unsubscribe from.

Third parties and age

Email sign-in needs no third-party service. If you choose Google sign-in, Google Identity Services verifies your identity. The local HTMX script is served from this site. Wallet connection is delegated to the user's chosen BCH wallet. No reviews or testimonials are displayed.

The prototype is not intended for children under 13.

Pagor is a software project for Bitcoin Cash. Settlement is public, details are private. This is not anonymity.
