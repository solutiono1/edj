Questions

The short answers.

The details that matter before you create your first private link.

Is pagor anonymous?

No. Settlement happens on a public blockchain. pagor keeps the amount out of the public story of a payment. It is not anonymity, and we do not claim it is.

Do I need a seed phrase?

No. You sign in with Google and choose a wallet passphrase. The two are combined on your device to derive your local signing key. Save the passphrase. You need it on another device.

Can anyone pay a private link?

Yes. Anyone with the link and a Bitcoin Cash wallet can pay it. The link carries the amount to the payer, but the amount is not published by pagor.

What is a disclosure proof?

A signed statement with a named reader, a threshold and an expiry. It shows that an account meets a threshold without revealing the balance. It is not a zero-knowledge proof.

Where is my data stored?

During the prototype, links, payroll drafts and proofs are kept in memory on the server and reset on restart. Your session token and key fingerprint stay in this browser.
