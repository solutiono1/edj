# LANDING DIRECTION (design pass)

## Audit of v2 landing
- Floating envelope loop: motion with no meaning
- Radial blue wash: color blob behind content
- Cursor glow: decorative effect, not product
- Uppercase tracked kicker: template label
- No real product proof, no sourced imagery, no developer story
- Too short to establish trust or show the product

## Direction (five lines)
- Product: private stablecoin payments and payroll on BCH for freelancers and small teams
- Character: exact, discreet, assured
- World: sealed envelope and clean ledger line, paper and ink with one blue wax detail
- Moments: one rendered Ed envelope, one real product preview showing Public/Private
- Signature: amount blurring into dots

## Passes applied
1. Spacing: 4px grid, equal card padding, aligned columns
2. Color: white and near-white neutrals, black ink, one blue accent #2A5CE6, no washes or glows
3. Icons: one 18px 1.5-stroke line set (inline Lucide paths)
4. Copy: short, sentence case, no em/en dashes, no hype, no unverified claims
5. Type: Nunito Sans display (closest open substitute for SF Pro Rounded), Inter body (closest open substitute for Neue Montreal), JetBrains Mono for code
6. Layout: asymmetric hero, text left, product preview right rising past fold, then proof, then developer surface
7. Details: nested radii, hairline borders, quiet meta text
8. Visuals: sourced Pexels imagery in proof cards, real app screenshot slots labeled for later capture
9. Motion: island nav transition, Public/Private blur toggle, short entrance fade and rise, no loops
